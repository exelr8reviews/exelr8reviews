SMS Consent & Opt-In Information

This page describes how end users consent to receive SMS messages sent by Exelr8Reviews.com on behalf of our local business partners.

How Consent Is Collected

End users opt in to receive SMS review request messages by verbally providing their phone number to one of our partner local businesses at the point of service — such as when booking an appointment, completing a service call, or checking out after a transaction.

The business collects the customer's phone number as part of their normal service process. By providing their phone number, the customer agrees to receive up to 2 automated SMS review request messages from Exelr8Reviews.com on behalf of that business.

Opt-In Disclosure

The following disclosure is presented to customers at the point of collection by our partner businesses, and is also publicly displayed here for transparency:

By providing your phone number to one of our partner businesses, you agree to receive up to 2 automated SMS review request messages from Exelr8Reviews.com on their behalf.

Message frequency: Maximum 2 messages per customer per visit.
Message & data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.
Reply HELP for assistance.

Privacy Policy: www.exelr8reviews.com/privacy.html
Terms & Conditions: www.exelr8reviews.com/terms.html

What Messages Are Sent

After a customer completes a transaction with one of our partner businesses, they may receive:

  • An initial SMS review request message (sent within 24 hours of service)
  • One follow-up SMS reminder (sent approximately 3 days later if no response)

No more than 2 messages are ever sent per customer per visit. Messages identify the sending business by name and always include opt-out instructions.

Opt-Out Instructions

To stop receiving messages at any time, reply STOP to any message.
You will receive one confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

For help, reply HELP or contact us at hello@exelr8reviews.com

Mobile Number Privacy

Mobile phone numbers collected through our partner businesses are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for any purpose other than delivering the review request messages described on this page.

For full details see our Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our SMS messaging or this consent policy, contact us at hello@exelr8reviews.com.
