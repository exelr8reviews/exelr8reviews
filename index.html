SMS Consent & Terms

How we collect consent and what customers can expect when they receive a text message from us.

✓ Consent Statement

How Customers Give Consent to Receive SMS Messages

Exelr8Reviews.com sends SMS messages on behalf of local businesses to their existing customers. Customers provide their phone number directly to the business at the point of service — for example when booking an appointment, completing a transaction, or receiving a service.

By providing their phone number to the business, customers consent to receive a follow-up SMS message from Exelr8Reviews.com on that business's behalf. The message asks the customer to share their experience by leaving a Google review.

No customer is contacted without first having an established relationship with the local business. We do not purchase, scrape, or obtain phone numbers from any third-party source.

📋 Process

How Consent Is Collected — Step by Step

1
Customer visits or hires a local business — such as a restaurant, HVAC company, dental office, salon, or auto repair shop.
2
Customer provides their phone number to the business at the point of booking, service, or checkout. This is standard practice for service businesses.
3
The business shares that phone number with Exelr8Reviews.com for the purpose of sending a review request on their behalf.
4
Exelr8Reviews.com sends one SMS message to the customer within 1–2 hours of their visit, asking them to leave a Google review for the business.
5
If no review is left after 3 days, one follow-up message may be sent. No further messages are sent after that.
💬 Example SMS Message Sent to Customers
"Hi [First Name]! Thanks for visiting [Business Name] today. We would love to hear how your experience was. Could you take 60 seconds to leave us a Google review? It means the world to us: [Google Review Link]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe."
🛑

How to Stop Receiving Messages

Customers can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. They will immediately be removed from all future messages. Customers may also reply HELP for assistance or contact us directly at hello@exelr8reviews.com.

📱 Message Frequency & Rates
  • Message frequency: A maximum of 2 messages are sent per customer per business visit — one initial request and one follow-up if no review is left.
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on the customer's mobile carrier and plan.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Messages are sent via a registered 10-digit long code (10DLC) number in compliance with CTIA guidelines.
🔒 Privacy & Data Use

Exelr8Reviews.com takes privacy seriously. Customer phone numbers are:

  • Used solely for the purpose of sending review requests on behalf of the local business
  • Never sold, rented, or shared with any third party
  • Stored securely and deleted upon termination of our agreement with the business
  • Never used for marketing or promotional purposes unrelated to the business visit

We comply with all applicable data protection laws including the TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) and CAN-SPAM Act.
