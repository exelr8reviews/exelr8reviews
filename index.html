2. Information We Collect

From Business Clients: Business name, address, contact information, email, phone number, and payment information.

From End Customers: First name, phone number, and email address when provided.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Sending automated SMS review requests on behalf of local businesses
  • Managing Google Business Profile reviews
  • Providing monthly reports to business clients
  • Processing payments for our services

4. SMS Messaging and TCPA Compliance

We comply fully with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act and CTIA messaging guidelines.

We never sell rent trade or share your personal information with third parties except trusted service providers needed to deliver our services or when required by law.

  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information

Contact us at hello@exelr8reviews.com to exercise these rights.
