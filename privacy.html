Privacy Policy

How Exelr8Reviews.com collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated: May 2025

1. Who We Are

Exelr8Reviews.com is a reputation management service that helps local businesses collect authentic Google reviews through automated SMS and email systems. We are based in Vero Beach, Florida and operate in the United States.

By using our services you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

From Business Clients:

  • Business name, address, and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Google Business Profile information
  • Payment information processed securely through third-party processors

From End Customers (via our clients):

  • First name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (when provided)

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, financial account details, or health information from end customers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • Sending automated SMS and email review requests on behalf of local businesses
  • Managing and monitoring Google Business Profile reviews
  • Providing monthly reports to our business clients
  • Communicating with business clients about their accounts
  • Processing payments for our services
  • Improving our services and customer experience

We do not use customer information for any purpose other than delivering the services described above.

4. SMS Messaging & TCPA Compliance

Exelr8Reviews.com sends SMS messages on behalf of local businesses to their existing customers. We comply fully with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and CTIA messaging guidelines.

Mobile Number Policy: Mobile phone numbers are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Message frequency: Maximum 2 messages per customer per business visit.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Customers provide consent by giving their phone number directly to the business at the point of service
  • Every message includes opt-out instructions
  • We honor all opt-out requests immediately

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party services including Twilio for SMS delivery and email platforms for follow-up messages. These providers only process data as needed to deliver our services.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We never sell customer data to marketers, advertisers, or any other third parties.

6. Data Security

  • Secure encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data transmission
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security reviews of our systems and processes
  • Secure deletion of customer data upon termination of service agreements

7. Data Retention

We retain customer contact information only for as long as necessary to deliver our services. When a business client terminates their agreement with us, all associated customer data is securely deleted within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

  • Opt Out of SMS: Reply STOP to any message at any time
  • Access: Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate information

To exercise any of these rights contact us at hello@exelr8reviews.com

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child please contact us immediately at hello@exelr8reviews.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify business clients of any significant changes by email. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Privacy questions? Contact us at hello@exelr8reviews.com
